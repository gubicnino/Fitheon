# FITHEON

**Project Title:** Fitheon

**Team Members:** Nino Gubič

## Project Description

**Fitheon is a mobile application that turns working out into a game.** Every completed workout rewards the user with items that can be used to build and upgrade their virtual gym. The more they work out, the bigger and richer their gym becomes.

The main goal of the application is **gamification of fitness**: we want to make regular exercise interesting, rewarding, and addictive, so that users return to working out because they want to, not because they have to.

### The Problem It Solves

Most people start exercising with good intentions, but their motivation quickly fades. Existing fitness applications are mostly just statistics trackers, which become boring after a few weeks.

**Fitheon takes a different approach.** Instead of plain numbers and graphs, users get a game in which every workout has a purpose: it provides rewards, unlocks something new, and expands their gym. Exercise is no longer a chore that needs to be checked off a list, but becomes a fun and exciting experience that users look forward to. Their gym grows alongside them.

### Who Is It For?

The application is intended for anyone who wants to exercise regularly but needs some extra motivation, especially gaming enthusiasts. In the future, we also want to expand it to groups of friends who could motivate and compete with each other.

### Why Is It Useful?

- **No Extra Work:** Workouts are recorded automatically, so users do not have to enter anything manually.
- **Instant Rewards:** Every workout is immediately reflected in the game.
- **Long-Term Motivation:** There is always another goal to achieve.

## Main Features

- **Automatic Workout Tracking:** The application automatically reads completed workouts from Apple Health.
- **Workout Rewards:** Every workout earns coins and experience points (XP). Longer and more intense workouts provide greater rewards.
- **Building Your Own Gym:** Users can purchase equipment with coins and customize their gym however they like.
- **Level Progression:** By collecting experience points, users level up and unlock increasingly larger gyms.
- **Return Summary:** Every time users open the application, they receive a summary of what they have earned since their last visit.

## Planned Technologies

- **Mobile Framework:** React Native (Expo)
- **Database and Backend:** Supabase
- **Health Data:** Apple HealthKit
